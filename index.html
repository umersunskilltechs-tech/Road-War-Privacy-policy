Privacy Policy for Road Rangers

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Road Rangers is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled within the Road Rangers mobile game application.

1. Information Collection and Storage

Road Rangers operates completely on-device. We do not collect, transmit, share, or sell your personal information or gameplay telemetry to any third party.

  • Game Progress & High Scores: All level stars, unlocked themes, coin balances, and garage upgrades are stored locally on your device via standard private application storage.
  • Player Profile & Photos: If you choose an optional profile photo from your device's photo library, the image is cropped and stored exclusively on your device's local sandboxed storage. It is never uploaded to any cloud server or network endpoint.

2. Permissions Requested

  • Photos / Storage Permission: Used solely to allow you to select a custom profile avatar from your photo library if you choose to do so. Road Rangers does not access any other photos or files.

3. Children's Privacy

Road Rangers does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. The game contains no third-party tracking, profiling, or behavioral advertising.

4. Third-Party Services

Road Rangers does not integrate third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or social tracking libraries.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@sunskilltechs.com.
